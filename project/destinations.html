Top Destinations

Five places every traveler should see at least once. Each card has quick facts and a link to their official tourism website.

Asia

Tokyo, Japan

Discover historic temples, neon skyscrapers, and world-class dining.

  • Language: Japanese
  • Currency: Japanese Yen (JPY)
  • Best time: March to May
Visit the official Japan Tourism website
Europe

London, England

Explore famous royal landmarks, top museums, and centuries of history.

  • Language: English
  • Currency: British Pound (GBP)
  • Best time: September to October
Visit the official Visit London Tourism website
North America

Los Angeles, USA

Relax on sunny coastlines and tour famous entertainment sights.

  • Language: English
  • Currency: US Dollar (USD)
  • Best time: June to September
Visit the official Los Angeles Tourism website
Africa

Cape Town, South Africa

Experience Table Mountain, scenic beaches, and rich local history.

  • Language: English, Afrikaans, Xhosa
  • Currency: South African Rand (ZAR)
  • Best time: November to March
Visit the official Cape Town Tourism website
South America

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

Enjoy famous tropical beaches, iconic mountain peaks, and lively samba culture.

  • Language: Portuguese
  • Currency: Brazilian Real (BRL)
  • Best time: December to March
Visit the official Rio de Janeiro Tourism website
