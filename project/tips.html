Travel Tips

Advice to help you get ready, save money, and pack the right things.

Getting Ready in 5 Steps

  1. Check that your passport is valid for at least 6 months after your trip.
  2. Look up visa rules on the State Department international travel page.
  3. Book flights 2 to 4 months early for better prices.
  4. Tell your bank you are traveling so your card is not blocked.
  5. Save copies of your passport and tickets on your phone.

Money Tips

  1. Pay in local currency
  2. Carry a little cash

Packing Checklist

  • Passport and ID
  • Phone charger and power adapter
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Basic medicine and first aid
  • Copies of travel documents
